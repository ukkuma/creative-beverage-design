# creative_beverage_design
A general-purpose skill for designing creative, visually appealing, and delicious beverages across alcoholic, zero-alcohol, tea, milk tea, fruit tea, yogurt, coffee, cocoa, sparkling, frozen, dessert, and hot drink categories.｜通用创意饮品设计Skill，覆盖酒精、0酒精、茶饮、奶茶、果茶、优酪乳、咖啡、可可、气泡、冰沙、甜品饮及热饮。

## 灵感与致谢 / Inspiration & Acknowledgements

特别感谢鱼头老师。

那天我只是随口分享，我会把没喝完、没那么好喝的饮料丢给AI，让它帮我重新设计。鱼头老师看完后说了一句：“搞个Skill吧”，然后默默“坐蹲”。

就是这句话，让我第一次认真去想：原来我平时和AI一起做的这些事情，也可以被整理成一个真正可复用的Skill。于是，我开始研究、第一次动手，也有了`creative_beverage_design`。

这是我的第一个Skill。谢谢鱼头老师，蹲到了。

Special thanks to 鱼头老师. A casual conversation about using AI to redesign unfinished or not-so-good drinks led to one simple suggestion: “搞个Skill吧” — followed by “坐蹲”. That small nudge made me realize that the way I was already working with AI could be turned into a reusable Skill. This became my first hands-on Skill project: `creative_beverage_design`.
