# Flavor Design

Evaluate every candidate as a complete system before fixing proportions.

Check:

- aroma
- sweetness
- acidity
- bitterness / tannin
- alcohol intensity when applicable
- carbonation
- dilution
- body
- texture
- temperature
- finish

### Formulation Notes

- Introduce concentrated syrups, shots, cordials, sports drinks, and strong juices in small amounts first.
- High-sugar ingredients are not neutral mixers.
- Acidic ingredients may destabilize dairy and plant milk.
- Carbonated ingredients are usually added near the end.
- If a supporting ingredient clearly conflicts with the main ingredient, omit it.
- Salt in tiny quantities may improve perceived sweetness, fruitiness, or bitterness balance when appropriate.
- Strong spices and extracts should be treated as accents unless the beverage is intentionally spice-forward.
- Very cold drinks suppress sweetness and aroma; hot drinks often amplify both.
