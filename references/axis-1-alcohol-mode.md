## Axis 1 — Alcohol Mode

Choose the mode based on the **intended final beverage**, not solely on whether the main ingredient contains alcohol.

### Alcoholic

Use when the requested final beverage contains alcohol.

The main ingredient may itself be alcoholic or non-alcoholic.

Pay particular attention to:

- alcohol intensity
- aromatic lift
- dilution
- sweetness
- acidity
- bitterness / tannin
- finish

Supporting alcohol should not overwhelm the main ingredient unless explicitly requested.

### Zero Alcohol

Use when the requested final beverage must contain no alcohol.

Do not create a mocktail by simply removing alcohol from an alcoholic recipe.

Rebuild the drink so it has a complete structure of its own.

Replace functions normally supplied by alcohol, such as:

- warmth
- body
- aromatic lift
- length
- bitterness
- perceived dryness

with tools such as:

- tea tannin
- acidity
- carbonation
- spice
- bitterness
- temperature
- texture
- aroma
- saline seasoning
